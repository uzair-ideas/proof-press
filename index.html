Drag the logo to reposition it. Both stores round the corners, so keep the mark inside the dashed shape. Play takes 512 × 512; the App Store takes 1024 × 1024 with no transparency. The guide is not exported.

Feature graphic · 1024 × 500 · Google Play only

Play crops this on some surfaces, so keep the words near the middle.

Google Play accepts 2–8 phone screenshots, PNG or JPEG, 16:9 or 9:16, every side between 320 and 3840 px, plus a 512 × 512 icon and a 1024 × 500 feature graphic. The App Store takes 1–10 screenshots per device size — 1290 × 2796 for the 6.9″ iPhone, 2048 × 2732 for the 13″ iPad — and a 1024 × 1024 icon with no transparency. Every size offered here sits inside those rules. In “one long image” mode the background is painted across the whole set and sliced, so upload the files in the numbered order or the picture breaks.
